Our story

A spiritual home for every generation.

Enam House exists to help people encounter Jesus Christ, grow into mature disciples and build strong, faith-filled families.

Our name represents home, belonging and shared purpose. We gather not only for a service, but to become a people shaped by God's Word and empowered by His Spirit.

Our foundation

What we believe

Jesus Christ

Jesus Christ is Lord and Saviour, and salvation is found in Him.

The Bible

Scripture is the inspired Word of God and our foundation for faith and life.

The Holy Spirit

God empowers believers through the Holy Spirit to live, serve and witness.

Prayer

Prayer changes lives, strengthens the church and draws us closer to God.

Family

Healthy, Christ-centred families help build healthy churches and communities.

Discipleship

Every believer is called to grow, serve and help others follow Jesus.

Our vision

To see lives transformed and families strengthened through the power of Jesus Christ.

  • Serve our community with love and integrity.
